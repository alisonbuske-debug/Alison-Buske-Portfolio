Work Samples & Case Studies

Case Study 1: Enterprise Oracle System Overhaul & Global Localization

Role: Lead Instructional Designer & EdTech Consultant | Tools: Assima, Clipchamp, Oracle, SAP GUI

The Challenge: A global hospitality enterprise relied on 800+ static screen-recording videos in English. Learners struggled in live software environments, resulting in high Helpdesk volume and low international engagement.

The Solution: Audited 800+ assets and developed a streamlined SME decision framework. Mapped out optimal system paths during storyboarding and replaced static videos with interactive Assima simulations featuring UI course wrappers, realistic narrative framing, and embedded decision points. Automated localization across 7 languages using custom voiceover synchronization workflows.

The Impact: Delivered 500+ localized assets, slashed seat time by nearly 50%, and scaled the framework to adjacent enterprise business units.

Case Study 2: Rapid Cybersecurity & Secure Coding Module

Role: Instructional Designer & Technical SME Collaborator | Tools: Articulate Rise 360, Interactive Knowledge Checks

The Challenge: Software developers needed rapid, impactful training on secure coding practices and penetration testing without interrupting tight development timelines.

The Solution: Partnered with cybersecurity SMEs to isolate high-priority vulnerability concepts. Built story-driven scenario activities where developers analyze code environments, identify security flaws, and mitigate risks in Articulate Rise through immersive decision trees.

The Impact: High developer engagement, minimal time-away-from-code, and actionable data collection for ongoing course refinement.

Case Study 3: Student-Centered Writing Workshop Architecture

Role: Associate Professor & Curriculum Innovator | Tools: Blackboard LMS, Narrative Strategy, Adult Learning Frameworks

The Challenge: Incoming student audiences needed targeted, foundational writing support to bridge skill gaps and build confidence in modern academic and professional communication.

The Solution: Designed and implemented a modular Writing Workshop model at Post University, tailored specifically to engage diverse student learners through practical narrative framing and interactive feedback loops.

The Impact: Established a permanent, department-wide focus on dedicated writing workshops for student audiences across the institution.

Learning Design Philosophy

  1. Anchor Learning in Authentic Narratives: Abstract rules and technical clicks don't stick; real-world stories, contextual challenges, and meaningful decision points do.
  2. Empower Adult Learners through Upskilling: Practical instruction should meet learners where they are, giving them immediate tools to write, communicate, and utilize software effectively.
  3. Prioritize Efficiency over Content Volume: Microlearning and scenario-driven decision trees respect learner time while maximizing real-world retention.
  4. Build for Sustainability: Ground course builds in robust frameworks like ADDIE and UbD while providing the coaching and documentation needed for internal teams to maintain them long-term.
